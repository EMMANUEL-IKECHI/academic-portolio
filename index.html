My Background

When I was a child, my parents often told me how fascinated I was with medicine. They said that wherever they kept drugs, I would find them and try to take them-innocently, without understanding what they were. I would even cry when not given any, believing they held some special power. Out of concern, my mother began keeping all medicines far from my reach. Looking back, I realize that this childhood curiosity was my earliest connection to science - a spark that would later grow into a lifelong passion for chemistry and human health.

That innocent fascination evolved into purpose. I wanted to understand what made medicines work, how molecules interact, and how chemistry could be used to design safer, more effective drugs. This motivation guided me through my undergraduate studies, where I earned a First-Class Honours degree in Chemistry from the Federal University of Technology, Owerri (FUTO), Nigeria.

Now a Research Assistant at Chemsolvers Research & Computational Laboratories, I explore the frontiers of computational and medicinal chemistry, integrating cheminformatics, molecular modeling, and artificial intelligence to accelerate drug discovery. My journey-from a curious child captivated by medicines to a researcher designing them-continues to inspire my vision of transforming curiosity into discovery that serves humanity.
