Key Research Projects

Generative AI-Driven Design and Machine Learning Prediction of Inhibitors Targeting Malaria - Plasmodium falciparum Dihydrofolate Reductase–Thymidylate Synthase

Overview: This project integrates generative AI (REINVENT framework), machine learning, and virtual screening to design and evaluate novel inhibitors against Plasmodium falciparum (malaria parasite).

Objectives:

Design & Generation

  • Generate novel antimalarial scaffolds using REINVENT.
  • Fine-tune the generative model on curated Plasmodium inhibitors.

Evaluation & Screening

  • Classify compounds using Machine Learning into active and inactive.
  • Perform virtual screening for drug-likeness and synthetic feasibility.

Integrating Graph Neural Networks and Molecular Docking to Predict Xylopia aethiopica Compounds’ Anti-Breast Cancer Activity

Overview: Combines Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) with molecular docking to identify potential inhibitors of the Human Estrogen Receptor Alpha (ERα) from natural compounds found in Xylopia aethiopica. This explores safer alternatives to current inhibitors for ER⁺ breast cancer treatment (PDB ID: 2IOK).

Objectives:

Modeling & Prediction

  • Model Training: GNN architecture for regression/classification of inhibitory potency.
  • Virtual Screening: Prediction of high-affinity ERα inhibitors.

Validation & Analysis

  • Docking Validation: Docking of top-ranked compounds with receptor structure (2IOK).

Computational QSAR Analysis of Bioactive Antimalarial Constituents from African Newbouldia laevis

Overview: Applies Quantitative Structure–Activity Relationship (QSAR) modeling to predict the biological activity of antimalarial compounds using machine learning models (Random Forest, XGBoost, etc.) based on molecular descriptors from RDKit/ECFPs.

Objectives:

Model Development

  • Develop QSAR models that predict Plasmodium falciparum (PDB ID: 7F3Y) inhibitory activity.
  • Compute 2D descriptors using RDKit and ECFP.

Training & Metrics

  • Train and compare machine learning algorithms for predictive analytics.
  • Evaluate models using various metrics (ROC-AUC, F1-score, etc.).
